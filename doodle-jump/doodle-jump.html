How to Play

🎯 Goal

Climb as high as you can by jumping from platform to platform!

🎮 Controls

Desktop: Use Left/Right Arrow Keys or A/D to move

Mobile: Use the on-screen buttons

🟦 Blue Platforms

Normal platforms - bounce on them to jump higher

🟠 Orange Platforms

Moving platforms - they move side to side for extra challenge

🟥 Red Platforms

Breakable platforms - they break after one jump!

🟩 Green Platforms (⭐)

Checkpoints - land on them to save your progress and respawn here if you fall

💡 Tips

• Go off one side of the screen to appear on the other

• Use checkpoints strategically - you can only respawn if you're 500+ points past your last checkpoint

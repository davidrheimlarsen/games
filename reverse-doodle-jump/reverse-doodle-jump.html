How to Play

🎯 Goal

Fall as far as you can while dodging obstacles!

🎮 Controls

Desktop: Use Left/Right Arrow Keys or A/D to move

Mobile: Use the on-screen buttons

🟥 Red Obstacles

Static obstacles - avoid these by moving left or right

🟠 Orange Obstacles

Moving obstacles - they move side to side for extra challenge

🟣 Purple Obstacles

Large obstacles - harder to dodge but worth more points

� Green Safe Zones (⭐)

Safe areas where you can take a break! They appear every 2500 points and act as checkpoints - you can respawn here if you fall off screen

�💡 Tips

• Go off one side of the screen to appear on the other

• The further you fall, the more points you earn

• Watch out for moving obstacles!

• Safe zones let you respawn if you're 200+ points past your last checkpoint

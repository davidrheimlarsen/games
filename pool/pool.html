How to Play

🎯 Goal

Sink all the colored balls into the pockets! Try to do it in as few shots as possible.

🎮 Controls

Aim & Power: Click on the white cue ball and drag away to aim. The further you drag, the more power!

Shoot: Release the mouse to take your shot.

⚪ Ball Physics

Balls bounce off cushions and each other realistically. Use angles to your advantage!

🎱 Balls

White: Cue ball (your ball)

Colored: Target balls (sink them all!)

💡 Tips

• Plan your shots carefully - use cushions for tricky angles

• Don't sink the cue ball or you'll get a penalty

• Watch the power meter for precise control

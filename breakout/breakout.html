How to Play

🎯 Goal

Break all the bricks by bouncing the ball off your paddle!

🎮 Controls

Desktop: Use Left/Right Arrow Keys or A/D to move paddle

Mobile: Use the on-screen buttons

🔵 Ball Physics

The ball angle changes based on where it hits the paddle - hit the edges for sharp angles!

🟦 Bricks

Blue: 1 hit | Green: 2 hits | Orange: 3 hits | Red: 4 hits

⚡ Power-ups

Wider Paddle: Makes your paddle wider

Multi-ball: Spawns extra balls

Slow Ball: Slows down all balls

Extra Life: Gives you an extra life

💡 Tips

• Catch power-ups by letting them fall on your paddle

• Use ball angle control to target specific bricks

• Some bricks require multiple hits to break
